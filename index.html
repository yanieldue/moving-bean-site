DAILY QUESTS

A goal for every day.

Simple movement goals each day for walking, running, and biking, in three difficulty tiers. Hit them to earn Beans and level up.

  • Finish a Normal or Hard quest and your buddy gains a star. Finish only Easy and it holds. Finish nothing and it loses one.
  • Keep 3 or more stars for 1.5x quest Beans, or fill all 7 for 2x.
  • Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets show your buddy and today's progress at a glance.
FIND BEAN

A new puzzle every day.

Spot Bean hidden in a busy pixel-art farm before your minute runs out. Each find earns 15 Beans, and you can see how your friends did.

FARMS

Grow a farm together.

Team up with friends on a shared farm. Pool your Beans to unlock new plots of land that boost the Beans everyone earns from quests, then fill the farm with animals and decorations.

STATS

Questions

Is Bean free?

Yes. Bean is free to download. Beans are earned by moving, and optional Bean Packs can also be bought in the app. If you would like to support us, you can leave an optional tip, which does not unlock anything in the app.

How does Bean track my activity?

Bean reads your steps, your walking, running, and biking distance, and your workouts from Apple Health, with your permission. It only reads this data to power your quests and your Stats page. It does not write anything back to Health.

When do my steps and distance update?

Opening Bean reads Apple Health straight away, so bringing the app up is the fastest way to see current numbers. In the background Bean updates as Health records new activity, at most once every five minutes, and the widget refreshes about every 15 minutes. Some lag is normal: iPhone saves steps to Health in batches rather than continuously, and a run or ride only appears once the workout has ended and the app that recorded it has synced to Health.

Why didn't my run or ride count?

Walking uses your step count, but running and biking only count distance from a recorded workout in Apple Health. If a run or ride didn't count, your tracking app is usually sending activity to Health without the workout itself. In Bean, open Settings, then Connect workout app, pick your app, and follow the steps. There are guides for Apple Watch, Strava, Nike Run Club, adidas Running, and Garmin.

How do I earn Beans?

By completing your daily quest tiers, finding Bean in the daily puzzle, claiming a free sample once a day, sharing a workout the first time each day for each activity, and watching optional ads, up to 10 a day. Your stars and a shared farm boost the Beans you earn from quests. If a friend signs up with your referral code, they get 30 Beans and so do you.

What are stars?

Your buddy has a star bar of up to 7 stars. Finishing a Normal or Hard quest gains a star that day, finishing only Easy holds the bar where it is, and a day with nothing finished loses one. With 3 or more stars you earn 1.5x Beans from quests, and a full bar earns 2x. Friends can see your stars.

Can I switch between Bean and Pea?

Yes. In Bean, open Settings, then Character, and tap the buddy you want. Switching is free, and you can change back any time.

Do I need friends to use it?

No. Bean works great solo. Friends and challenges are optional if you want a little extra push.

What can my friends see?

Friends you add can see your buddy and its stars, today's quest progress, your weekly and monthly movement totals, how you did in each day's Find Bean puzzle, and your one-second moments and highlight reels. Moments are backed up to your account in private storage, never posted publicly, and you can delete any moment in the app. Deleting your account removes all of it.

What happens if I get a new phone?

Sign in with the same account and your progress, Beans, cosmetics, and moments are restored.

How do I delete my account?

In Bean, open Settings, then Delete account. This permanently removes your progress, Beans, cosmetics, and moments. Your Apple Health data is not affected.

What platforms is it on?

Bean is on iPhone, with widgets for your Home Screen and Lock Screen. Download it on the App Store.
