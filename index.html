A little buddy you dress up by moving.

Bean turns your everyday steps, walks, runs, and rides into daily quests to complete, outfits to unlock, and friendly competition with friends. No likes. No follower counts. Just movement.

What you get

Small, satisfying reasons to keep moving, built around a character you will actually care about.

Meet Pea and Bean

Pick your buddy and make it yours. Bean reads the steps and distance already in Apple Health to power your daily quests, no extra tracking required.

Daily quests

Simple movement goals each day for walking, running, and biking. Hit them to keep your streak going and earn rewards.

Earn Lettuce, dress up

Movement earns Lettuce, the in-app currency. Spend it on outfits and cosmetics that change how your buddy looks.

One-second moments

Capture a one-second clip from a walk or run and stitch your favorites into a highlight reel of your week outside.

Compete with friends

Start or join a movement challenge and see who moves the most that week. Friendly competition, no stakes.

Healthy by design

No likes, no comments, no follower counts. Bean keeps the focus on your movement and your friends, not on chasing numbers.

Questions

Is Bean free?

Yes. Bean is free to download. Lettuce is always earned by moving, and you can also watch a short ad for a little more.

How does Bean track my activity?

Bean reads your steps and walking, running, and biking distance from Apple Health, with your permission. It only reads this data to power your quests. It does not write anything back to Health.

Do I need friends to use it?

No. Bean works great solo. Friends and challenges are optional if you want a little extra push.

What platforms is it on?

Bean is on iPhone. Download it on the App Store.
