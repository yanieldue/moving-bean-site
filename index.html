A little buddy you dress up by moving.

Bean turns your everyday steps, walks, runs, and rides into daily quests to complete, outfits to unlock, and friendly competition with friends. No likes. No follower counts. Just movement.

Download on the App StoreFREE ON IPHONE

What you get

Small, satisfying reasons to keep moving, built around a character you will actually care about.

Meet Bean and Pea

You start as Bean. Unlock outfits in Bean and Pea styles and dress your buddy however you like. Quests run on the steps and distance already in Apple Health, no extra tracking required.

Daily quests

Simple movement goals each day for walking, running, and biking, in three difficulty tiers. Hit them to earn Lettuce and level up.

Earn Lettuce, dress up

Movement earns Lettuce, the in-app currency. Spend it on outfits and cosmetics that change how your buddy looks.

One-second moments

Capture a one-second clip from a walk or run, stitch your favorites into a highlight reel, and share them with the friends you add.

Compete with friends

Start or join a walking, running, or biking challenge with friends and see who moves the most. You pick how long it runs. Free to enter, no stakes.

Share a Garage

Team up with friends in a shared Garage. Pool your Lettuce to upgrade your ride, and every upgrade boosts the Lettuce everyone earns from quests.

Limited-time missions

Special events with a ladder of daily movement goals. Every stage you reach unlocks a cosmetic you keep for good.

Widgets

Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets show your buddy and today's quest progress at a glance.

Healthy by design

No likes, no comments, no follower counts. Bean keeps the focus on your movement and your friends, not on chasing numbers.

Questions

Is Bean free?

Yes. Bean is free to download and there is nothing to buy inside the app, including Lettuce. If you would like to support us, you can leave an optional tip, which does not unlock anything in the app.

How does Bean track my activity?

Bean reads your steps, your walking, running, and biking distance, and your workouts from Apple Health, with your permission. It only reads this data to power your quests. It does not write anything back to Health.

Why didn't my run or ride count?

Walking uses your step count, but running and biking only count distance from a recorded workout in Apple Health. If a run or ride didn't count, your tracking app is usually sending activity to Health without the workout itself. In Bean, open Settings, then Connect workout app, pick your app, and follow the steps. There are guides for Apple Watch, Strava, Nike Run Club, adidas Running, and Garmin.

How do I earn Lettuce?

By completing your daily quest tiers, claiming a free sample once a day, sharing a workout the first time each day for each activity, and watching optional ads, up to 10 a day. A shared Garage boosts the Lettuce you earn from quests. If a friend signs up with your referral code, they get 30 Lettuce and you get 30 once they finish their first quest.

Do I need friends to use it?

No. Bean works great solo. Friends and challenges are optional if you want a little extra push.

What can my friends see?

Friends you add can see your buddy, today's quest progress, your weekly and monthly movement totals, and your one-second moments and highlight reels. Moments are backed up to your account in private storage, never posted publicly, and you can delete any moment in the app. Deleting your account removes all of it.

What happens if I get a new phone?

Sign in with the same account and your progress, Lettuce, cosmetics, and moments are restored.

How do I delete my account?

In Bean, open Settings, then Delete account. This permanently removes your progress, Lettuce, Shards, cosmetics, and moments. Your Apple Health data is not affected.

What platforms is it on?

Bean is on iPhone, with widgets for your Home Screen and Lock Screen. Download it on the App Store.
