A little buddy that grows when you move.

Bean turns your everyday steps, walks, runs, and rides into a character that grows, daily quests to complete, and friendly wagers with friends. No likes. No follower counts. Just movement.

What you get

Small, satisfying reasons to keep moving, built around a character you will actually care about.

A character that grows

Meet Pea and Bean. Your buddy reflects what you do in the real world, powered by the steps and distance already in Apple Health.

Daily quests

Simple movement goals each day for walking, running, and biking. Hit them to keep your streak going and earn rewards.

Earn Lettuce, dress up

Movement earns Lettuce, the in-app currency. Spend it on outfits and cosmetics that change how your buddy looks.

One-second moments

Capture a one-second clip from a walk or run and stitch your favorites into a highlight reel of your week outside.

Wager with friends

Challenge a friend to a movement bet. Put some Lettuce on the line and let the more active week win.

Healthy by design

No likes, no comments, no follower counts. Bean is built to motivate you, not to keep you scrolling.

Questions

Is Bean free?

Yes. Bean is free to download. You earn Lettuce by moving, and there are optional ways to get more, including watching a short ad or an in-app purchase.

How does Bean track my activity?

Bean reads your steps and walking, running, and biking distance from Apple Health, with your permission. It only reads this data to power your quests. It does not write anything back to Health.

Do I need friends to use it?

No. Bean works great solo. Friends and wagers are optional if you want a little extra push.
