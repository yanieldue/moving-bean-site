Delete your Bean account

Bean is published by Forwardslash LLC. This page explains how to delete your Bean account, what is deleted with it, and what happens if you cannot open the app.

Delete your account from inside the app

  1. Open Bean and go to the Profile tab.
  2. Tap the gear icon in the top right to open Settings.
  3. Tap My Account.
  4. Tap Delete account at the bottom of the page.
  5. Type DELETE in the box to confirm.
  6. Tap DELETE MY ACCOUNT. The deletion runs immediately.

Deleting your account signs you out on every device. It cannot be undone, and an account cannot be restored once it is deleted.

If you cannot open the app

Email support@moving-bean.com from the address your account is registered to, and ask for your account to be deleted. We will confirm by email once it is done.

If you signed in with Apple or Google and are not sure which address that is, tell us your Bean username and we will help you identify the account before deleting anything.

What is deleted

Deleting your account permanently removes all of the following:

  • Your account record and your sign-in credentials.
  • Your username, display name and profile.
  • Your activity history, including the daily step, running and cycling totals derived from Apple Health or Health Connect.
  • Your quest progress, levels, streaks, stars and Bean balance.
  • Your cosmetic items, and any items you had equipped.
  • Every one-second moment you have recorded, including the video files.
  • Your friend connections and any pending friend requests.
  • Your challenge history and your Treehouse membership.
  • Your notification settings and device tokens.

What is kept, and for how long

Nothing is kept on a retention timer. Deletion removes your account row, every record linked to it, and the stored video files in one pass, so no copy of your personal data is retained afterwards.

Two narrow exceptions apply, neither of which identifies you. Purchase records held by Apple or Google under their own terms are not ours to delete, and you should contact them directly about those. And aggregate, anonymous usage counts that can no longer be linked back to you or to any account may remain in our analytics.

Deleting some data without deleting your account

You can delete an individual one-second moment at any time from the Moments tab, which removes the video file as well as the record of it. Your account and the rest of your progress are left untouched.

To revoke Bean's access to your health data without deleting your account, open the Settings app on iPhone and go to Privacy & Security > Health, or open Health Connect on Android and go to App permissions.

For any other partial deletion request, email support@moving-bean.com.
